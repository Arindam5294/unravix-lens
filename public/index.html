How it works

Three steps, one knot

01

Snap

Photograph one handwritten solution in good light. Lens shrinks it and strips location data on your phone.

02

Segment

Meta SAM 3.1 finds the lines of working. Muse Spark fills any line SAM groups or misses, and Lens snaps each box to the ink.

03

Untie

Muse Spark finds the first wrong step, names the misconception, gives the fix and three check questions.
