    How it works

    Two ways in, one clear answer

    Type any doubt, photograph a question or a textbook passage, or snap your own working. Any subject, from Class 1 sums to PhD proofs.

    Muse Spark works out the subject and your level. For working, Meta SAM 3.1 maps every line and Lens snaps each box to the ink.
